# Play Store Listing Metadata

Use these descriptions when submitting to the Google Play Console. They have been written to be professional and avoid being flagged by automated profanity filters.

## Short Description (Max 80 characters)
> Share files anonymously and directly from your phone via Tor, Ngrok, or WiFi.

## Long Description
**Drop Any Media Now** is a privacy-focused file sharing utility that turns your Android device into a secure, direct-host web server.

Stop relying on third-party cloud providers that scan your data. With this app, your files never leave your device until the recipient starts the download. Whether you need to send a large video to a friend across the room or a sensitive document to someone across the world, you can do it without a middleman.

### SECURE TUNNELING OPTIONS
*   **Tor Onion Services**: Bypass firewalls and stay completely anonymous with automatically generated .onion addresses.
*   **Ngrok Tunnels**: Quickly create a secure public URL to share files over the internet without complex router settings.
*   **Local Network (UPnP)**: High-speed sharing over Wi-Fi with automatic NAT port forwarding.

### PRIVACY AT ITS CORE
*   **No Cloud Storage**: We never see your files because we don't have a server to store them on.
*   **No Accounts**: Start sharing immediately without providing an email address or phone number.
*   **No Tracking**: We don't use analytics or tracking scripts. Your activity is your business.
*   **Open Source**: Our code is transparent and open for audit under the GPLv3 license.

### POWERFUL TOOLS
*   **Folder Sharing**: Share entire directories, not just single files.
*   **Custom Ports**: Full control over your server configuration.
*   **Background Operation**: Keep your server running while you use other apps via a stable foreground service.
*   **Password Protection**: Add an extra layer of security to your shared links.
