# Play Store Listing Metadata

Use these descriptions when submitting to the Google Play Console. They have been written to be professional, utility-focused, and avoid flags from automated profanity or "dark web" filters.

## Short Description (Max 80 characters)
> Turn your phone into a private web server. Direct sharing via secure tunnels.

## Long Description (Max 4000 characters)
**Drop Any Media Now (D.A.M.N.)** is a high-performance utility that transforms your Android device into a private, professional-grade web server. Share files and folders directly from your phone without relying on third-party cloud providers, data collection, or middleman servers.

### SOVEREIGN HOSTING – NO ROOT REQUIRED
Maintain full control over your data. By hosting directly from your device, you ensure that your files remain private and accessible only to those you choose. Support for industry-standard tunnels like Cloudflare, Ngrok, and Tor is built-in and entirely optional.

### BYPASS NETWORK RESTRICTIONS
Mobile carriers often place devices behind strict firewalls and Carrier-Grade NAT (CGNAT), which usually makes hosting impossible. D.A.M.N. provides professional-grade tunneling to ensure your content is reachable globally:
• **Web Tunnels (Cloudflare & Ngrok)**: Create secure public paths instantly. Even on restrictive LTE/5G networks, these integrations provide a stable URL for global access.
• **NAT Traversal (UPnP)**: Automatically configure your home router to open necessary ports, providing a direct connection without manual setup.

### PRIVATE CONNECTIVITY VIA SECURE PATHS
Privacy is at the core of the D.A.M.N. architecture.
• **End-to-End Security**: Leverage secure protocols to ensure your connection is private and protected from local network eavesdropping.
• **Tor Tunneling**: Optionally route your server through a private .onion address to bypass restrictive network filters and ensure a secure, direct-to-recipient path.
• **Direct Path**: Your files go straight from your phone to the recipient. We never see, scan, or store your data because we don't have a central server.

### PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPER TOOLS
• **Integrated PHP Engine**: Includes a lightweight, native PHP engine to render scripts and serve dynamic content directly from your device.
• **Secure Storage Access**: Built on the Android Storage Access Framework (SAF), allowing you to host individual files or massive directories instantly without copying data.
• **Live Diagnostics**: Monitor every request in real-time with a verbose console—perfect for debugging webhooks or tracking active transfers.
• **Customizable Environment**: Full control over your hosting settings, including custom ports (1024–65535).

### BUILT FOR RELIABILITY
• **Foreground Service**: Runs as a high-priority process with a persistent notification, ensuring your server stays active during background use.
• **Automatic Resumption**: Optional "Start at Boot" ensures your server resumes immediately after a device restart.
• **Resource Optimized**: Engineered to minimize CPU and battery footprint while the server is idle.

### WHY CHOOSE D.A.M.N.?
• **Eliminate the Cloud**: Keep your data on your device. Stop uploading private files to central servers just to share them.
• **Developer Friendly**: Test mobile-first sites, webhooks, or simple APIs on real hardware without a laptop.
• **Private Media Sharing**: Share high-resolution photos and videos without compression or privacy compromises.

### GET STARTED IN SECONDS:
1. Select a file or folder using the secure system picker.
2. Choose your connection method (Local, NAT, or Secure Tunnel).
3. Tap **START SERVER**.
4. Share your generated URL and host on your own terms.
