# Privacy Policy for Drop Any Media Now (D·A·M·N)

**Last Updated: August 30, 2026**

D·A·M·N ("Drop Any Media Now") is provided by **Bryce Hudson** as an Open Source application. This SERVICE is provided at no cost and is intended for use "as is."

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
Our core philosophy is absolute privacy. We do not operate any backend servers that store your data.

*   **No Personal Data Collection**: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, account details, or contact lists. 
*   **Media Access vs. Collection**: The app requires access to your device's storage/media to function. While the app **accesses** these files to serve them to your intended recipients, it **never transmits** these files to the developer or any unauthorized third party.
*   **No Analytics**: We do not use any third-party analytics, SDKs, or tracking software. 
*   **Local Hosting**: D·A·M·N acts as a local server on your device. Your files stay on your hardware until the moment they are downloaded by the person you share the link with.

## 2. Permissions and Their Purpose
To provide its service, the app requests the following permissions:
*   **INTERNET**: Required to host the local server and establish secure tunnels.
*   **READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA**: Required to access the specific files or folders you explicitly choose to share.
*   **POST_NOTIFICATIONS**: Used to maintain the server's status in the background via a foreground service.
*   **RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED**: Optionally used to restart the server upon device reboot (user-configurable).

## 3. Third-Party Services
If you enable external sharing features, the following services are involved. We do not control these services, and their respective privacy policies apply:
*   **Tor (Guardian Project)**: For anonymous .onion addresses. [Tor Privacy Policy](https://www.torproject.org/about/privacy/)
*   **Ngrok**: For secure public tunnels. [Ngrok Privacy Policy](https://ngrok.com/privacy)
*   **Cloudflare**: For tunnel connectivity. [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)

## 4. Children’s Privacy
These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Since no data is collected, there is no risk of such data being stored on our systems.

## 5. Data Deletion
Because D·A·M·N does not collect or store any user data on its own servers, there is no user data to delete. If you have any concerns regarding the data the app accesses on your device, you may contact the developer directly.
